Center Scrimmage

A real 5-on-5 game. You're the center with the black ring. Skate with the mouse. Get the puck, pass it, shoot it. Get close to whoever has it and click them to battle for it. When the other team has it, figure out if you're F1, F2 or F3 and be in the right spot. Lose it in their end? Backcheck. Coach points come from being in the right place, not from goals.
