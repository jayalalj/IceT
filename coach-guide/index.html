Coach Guide

How to design a play, try it the way the kids will, and share it with the team. About ten minutes the first time, and a few minutes after that.

Open the Play Designer →

1Get into coach mode

Open the link Janaka sent you (it ends in ?coach=…), or press Coach login at the bottom of the home page and type the coach code. You only do this once per device.

You'll know it worked when you see the red ✎ Feedback and Coach ✕ buttons in the bottom-left corner, and a Coach preview section on the home page.

2Design the play

On the home page, open Play Designer from Coach preview. The rink is our end on the left, with the neutral zone to the right. Orange are our forwards, navy are our D, and grey X's are the other team.

  1. Name it. Use something the kids will recognise, like "D-zone faceoff loss".
  2. Pick a starting setup under Start from: Faceoff, Breakout, D-zone coverage or Neutral zone. This is the Set step. Drag anyone who isn't where you want them, including the puck and the other team.
  3. Press + Add step. Now drag each player, and the puck, to where they are at the end of that step. Faded players with arrows show where they started, so you can see the movement.
  4. Write one line in What happens in step N, such as "LD passes up the wall to LW." That's the caption the kids read.
  5. Repeat for each step. Three or four steps is the sweet spot, and nine is the maximum.
  6. Optional: open Player jobs and write one sentence for each position. Kids see the job for the position they pick. For defensive plays, tick Show the house & shot lanes.
Curves: players move in straight lines between steps. To go behind the net or around a player, add an extra step at the turn.
Moving the puck: drag the puck to the player who receives the pass. Put it just in front of them, not on top, so it's easy to grab next time.

3Check it the way the kids will

  • ▶ Preview plays the animation right in the designer, with your captions.
  • Test it opens the real player page in a new tab. Pick a position, press Watch, then Play it and skate it yourself. It grades each step, so if a spot is hard to reach in time, move it closer or add a step.
  • Changes? Go back to the designer tab, adjust, and press Test it again.

4Save and share

  • Save keeps the play on this device and browser only. Reopen it later from My saved plays.
  • Copy share link copies a link that contains the whole play. Paste it into a text or email. Anyone who opens it gets the full Watch and Play-it page, and they don't need the coach code.
Heads up: saved plays don't sync between devices. A play saved on your laptop won't show up on your phone. The share link always works everywhere, so copy it when you're done.

5Get it on the team site

  1. Text or email the share link to Janaka with the name you want on the home page.
  2. It goes into Coach preview first, so other coaches can try it and send feedback.
  3. When you're happy, tell Janaka "publish it" and it moves to Team plays for everyone.

Need it for practice tonight? You can also send the share link straight to the team chat. It works right away.

6Send feedback on any page

On any page, press ✎ Feedback. It already notes which page, play and position you're looking at. Type what should change, like "Strong-side exit, step 3: LD closer to the boards", and send it.

Quick answers

Can I edit a play someone shared with me?
Yes. Open the link, then press ✎ Edit in designer (coaches only). Save or share it as a new link. The old link keeps showing the old version.
Does it work on a phone or iPad?
Yes. Turn it sideways and drag with your finger. A laptop is easier for designing; phones are great for testing.
I messed up a step.
Select the step and press Delete step, or just drag players back. Picking a new setup under Start from starts over.
Who can see the designer?
Only devices in coach mode. Everyone else sees "Coming soon". Share links are open to anyone who has them.
