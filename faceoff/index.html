    Coach's rules
    • Win it away from the net. Best draw on the strong side goes toward the corner or straight to LW, as far from our net as possible.
    • LD hugs the wall on exits. As we move up ice, LD stays close to the boards in case the puck comes back.
    • Move on the drop. If you wait to react, you'll be too late.
    • Keep it out of the house. Nobody open in front of our net. RD stays there until we have it.
    • Don't leave early. Don't leave the zone without possession.
    • Don't panic. Tie-up, find the wall, move it up.
